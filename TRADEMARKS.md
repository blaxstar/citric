# Trademarks

The citric source code is licensed under Apache-2.0, which covers using,
modifying and redistributing it. The license doesn't grant rights to the
names or marks associated with it (section 6), so:

- You may say a fork or product is "based on citric" or "built with citric".
- Don't use the BLAXSTAR name, or the citric icons in `web/public`, to brand
  a fork or derived product, or in a way that suggests BLAXSTAR LLC endorses
  it. Replace the icons and the footer credit in your fork.
